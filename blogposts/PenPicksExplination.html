I was hanging out in the Penaddict stream the other day, and they were discussing different ballpoint pens.

It so happens that I got a work email regarding planning for March Madness tournament. As i so often (probably to much) do, I asked if Brad would do a ballpoint pens bracket in March. Understandably, he replied that would be a lot of work, and he wasn't sure.

So I made my own webpage to take bracket submittals. This was more of a programming exercise than a pen picking one, and I hope to use it for my own families March Madness competition. It takes the submissions, and collects them into a spreadsheet where I can sort and assign points.

If you have ideas for improvements feel free to submit feedback!
