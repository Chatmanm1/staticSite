Taken from [Style is consistent constraint — Steph Ango](https://stephango.com/style), and I'm sure many other sources. A good way to understand and have guiding principles that help you reflect on who you are in the world. There are many other individuals who have similar ideas, I've also seen them referred to as 'Mantra.' Below are some of mine. ### What are my *Style Choices* - I prefer making my own version of things if possible. - If given the choice, I'll do things the perceived more difficult way. - Objects have spirit, imbued in them by use. - See [Thoughts On: Religion](https://matchamakes.net/blogposts/unwritten) - I enjoy making things more than consuming them. - Less is better than more. 260911 updated.
