There are many other individuals who have similar ideas, I've also seen them referred to as 'Mantra.' 


### What are my *Style Choices*

1. I don't wear logos, unless paid to do so.
2. I prefer making my own version of things if possible. 
3. Less things more meaning.
4. If given the choice, I'll do things the perceived more difficult way.
5. Objects have spirit, imbued in them by use.
	1. See [Thoughts On: Religion](https://matchamakes.net/blogposts/unwritten)
6. Writing things down or logging them is the key form of memory.
7. I enjoy making things more than consuming them. 
8. Food is a fuel
