# Thoughts On - Main ### Preamble When navigating through life, frequently I find myself having strong emotions about random topics, things, or ideas. Per [Merlin Mann's](http://wisdom.limo/) advice, writing something every day is important. The quantity, and subject matter isn't super important. The fact that you spend time creating something and thinking about a topic is the benefit. At least, that is my interpretation. [Derek Sivers](https://sive.rs/dj) recommends having journals for topics for things you think of frequently. I keep all my daily thoughts in my [[One big text file]] and can reference back to that when I want to know when/if I though about a topic before. I average about 32 entries per day in that file, so there are plenty of thoughts. I wanted to take on this as a challenge for November in 2025. Last year I attempted to do [NANOWRIMO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Novel_Writing_Month), and was not successful. I did get something started, but didn't finish. I'm hoping the openness of this [[Challenge to myself]] will allow me to complete it every day, or at least feel some sort of success at the end. ### Topics Please find below the topics linked to their respective posts. These were generated randomly. I just took the first generated list and am going with it. I do reserve the right to work on these in any order, and have varying lengths, and lack of structure.
