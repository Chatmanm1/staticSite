Making

Woodworking

I recently attending Woodworking in America and had a blast meeting different vendors, and the teams from Fine Woodworking and Woodsmith

Documenting my projects has become somewhat of a habit on Instagram , my complaints with that platform, and the social internet aside, it 's been fun. Through that and the Fine Woodworking Discord channel, I've learned a lot of techniques and met many interesting people.

A month (maybe three) ago, I cut down some 2x4 materials into small box parts. The original idea was to make pen boxes. After realizing just how much of a pain it would be to hand cut miters perfectly for 20 boxes, I decided that I'd instead use the parts as a way to experiment with design. I've done 6 so far. Three are carved, and three boxes are painted and covered with washi paper. It's been a great way to try new ways of box fabrication. My favorite so far is a red and black one that I've been storing my cheap carving tools in.
